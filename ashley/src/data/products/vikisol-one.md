Vikisol One is the ecosystem's workforce and HR platform - built for organizations to actually run the day-to-day of managing people, not just store their records.

It handles the operational reality of a workforce: attendance, payroll, onboarding a new hire and offboarding one who's leaving, keeping employee records straight, and giving a team a real organization chart instead of a spreadsheet.

Access to Vikisol One is organization-controlled. It isn't a public sign-up product - an organization's employees get access through their organization, not through this website.
