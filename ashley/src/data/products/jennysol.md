JennySol is the Vikisol ecosystem's central AI layer - not a chatbot bolted onto one product, but a shared assistant meant to work across all of them as they connect to it.

It runs real conversations today, and it can already act with real consequence in one connected product: through a controlled connector, it can search Arena's job openings and, with your explicit approval at the moment it matters, apply to one on your behalf. That approval step is not a formality - a write action a model proposes is never carried out automatically. It is proposed, you review it, and only then does it happen.

Underneath, JennySol is deliberately built so it never reaches directly into another product's database. Every product it connects to - Arena today, more in the future - is reached through the same narrow, controlled interface, so what it can do in one place stays contained to what that product explicitly allows.
