Arena is built around a simple idea: most useful things start with someone stating a real need, not with a resume sitting in a database.

It is not a resume database and it is not a feed to scroll. It is closer to a marketplace of real needs - a job to fill, a project to staff, a skill to find - where the right people can actually see it and respond, a conversation can happen, and an outcome follows. Every outcome adds to a real, growing professional or company identity on the platform, which is what makes the next response faster and better matched.

Arena is not a hiring portal Vikisol built only for itself. It's a real, independent product - companies post their own real openings and opportunities on it today.
